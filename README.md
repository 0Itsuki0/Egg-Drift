## Features

### 🌀 Radial Physics

Everything pushes outward from the center like a spinning cone arena. A fresh
twist on merge puzzle gameplay.

### 📱 Motion Controls

Tilt your phone to drift creatures into strategic collisions and chain merges.

### ✨ Endless Evolution

Combine adorable capsule creatures into bigger and rarer forms before space runs
out.
